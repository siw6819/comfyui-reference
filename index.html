── LTX 2.3 · Dev vs Distilled
DEV DISTILLED
What it is Full research/development model. Highest fidelity output. Trained to mimic dev in far fewer steps. Faster, slightly softer.
Steps needed 40–80 steps typical 4–8 steps typical
Scheduler Standard (Euler, DPM++ etc.) Must use distilled-aware scheduler (e.g. LCM, TCD, or model's recommended cfg=1)

⚠ Distilled models used with wrong scheduler / CFG = blurry, washed-out frames. Always check the model card for recommended settings.
── LoRA Ranks Explained

Rank controls how many parameters the LoRA uses per layer. Think of it as the "resolution" of the adaptation — higher rank can capture more complex changes but costs more VRAM and file size.

r4 — tiny r16 — light r32 — common r64–128 — heavy r256+ — very large
r4 – r8
Minimal
Tiny file, lowest VRAM impact. Style nudges only. Won't learn complex subjects.
r16 – r32
Common sweet spot
Balanced. Good for most style and concept LoRAs. What most community models use.
r64 – r128
Heavy
High fidelity. Better for faces, complex motions, detailed character training.
r256 – r512
Very large
Approaching full fine-tune territory. Often used in video model LoRAs like LTX.
